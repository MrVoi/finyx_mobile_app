**Finyx** is a comprehensive smart financial management application designed for both individuals and businesses to take control of their finances with ease. The app tracks income and expenses, visualizes spending breakdowns with interactive charts, provides an intelligent AI Financial Advisor, manages wallets/bills, and supports multi-language localization (English and Arabic) with dark and light themes.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 💰 **Dual Account Modes**:
  - **Individual**: Tracks personal bills (electricity, water, internet, food, shopping, zakat, car, entertainment, etc.) with salary tracking.
  - **Business**: Manages revenues, expenses, payroll/salaries, shipping, taxes, loans, licenses, and interest.
- 📊 **Interactive Expense Charts**: Real-time spending distribution with dynamic percentages and category color coding.
- 💳 **Wallet & Bill Management**: Quick add, edit, and deletion of custom recurring bills and expenses.
- 🤖 **AI Financial Assistant**: Integrated financial tips, Zakat calculator (2.5% against Nisab), and 20% savings rule analysis.
- 🌐 **Full Bilingual Support**: Instant toggle between English and Arabic (RTL layout support).
- 🌓 **Themes**: Seamless switching between Light and Dark mode.
- 👤 **Profile & Security**: Manage personal details, salary, and credentials with Firebase authentication or demo accounts.
